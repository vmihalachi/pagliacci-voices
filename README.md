# Mister Pagliacci's voices

The recorded lines of Mister Pagliacci, made with ElevenLabs:

- `Voice/<lang>/<voice>/<id>.m4a` and `Voice/manifest.json`, bundled by the app (this repository is the `voices` submodule of pagliacci, shared by its Mac and Windows apps).
- `review/<lang>.html`, a page for friends to listen through and flag what sounds off: https://vmihalachi.github.io/pagliacci-voices/

Everything here is generated by `deskling/tools/voice.py` in pagliacci (`render`, `retake`, `site`, `publish`); don't edit it by hand.
